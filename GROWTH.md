# Engineering growth — evidence before popularity

Snapshot from GitHub on 2026-11-16: 32 public repositories, 88 total stars, 4 forks, 8 followers. Totals include forked repositories and are not a company rating. No universal GitHub hiring rank or guaranteed follower/star growth exists.

## Six-week plan

1. Focus on three flagships: runnable backend, real product and bilingual roadmap. Preserve other repositories and label prototypes honestly.
2. Add tested real authentication, persistence and typed responses before calling the backend production-ready.
3. Publish a verifiable product case study: problem, your role, tradeoffs, screenshots, measured outcomes and limitations. Obtain permission before publishing customer or personal data.
4. Complete the four advanced labs and deliver one tested mini-project per track: [advanced engineering labs](https://majid-alsakani.github.io/developer-roadmap/learn/advanced-labs.html).
5. Make a useful narrowly scoped external contribution; follow the maintainer's policies and avoid mass unsolicited pull requests.
6. Publish a reproducible walkthrough and invite optional feedback. Measure useful issues resolved, external contributors and successful first runs.

Never buy, trade, automate or fabricate stars, followers, reviews or historical contributions. Hidden keyword stuffing is not a reliable SEO strategy. Track genuine results monthly; do not invent impact percentages. Enable GitHub two-factor authentication; it was reported disabled at audit time. Never share tokens or recovery codes.

The backend demo login still lacks credential verification and persistence; the labs are initial practical units, not a complete curriculum. X publishing awaits four safely stored keys and scheduler activation; LinkedIn is not connected. Product claims need fresh verification.
